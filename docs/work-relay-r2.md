## Per-plan relay

The renderer, gate and T3 provider reader share one plan table. Wire plan
`claude` resolves to `claudeAgent`. The Mac reports each built-in instance's
connection and advertised model IDs separately. The picker keeps reported models
selectable while a connected Mac is offline and sends the exact plan and model
with the selection revision. Each admitted receipt keeps the plan for recovery,
stop and transcript chrome.

An owner turn uses the per-plan account snapshot and the single per-Mac switch.
Scheduled turns use a separate standing grant for the plan, assistant and Mac.
Antigravity reports unknown usage and maps auto-accept-edits to supervised
`default`. Claude and Codex use their native modes under the gate ceiling.
